Souvera Workspace – Mail, calling and protection in one app

Souvera Workspace brings together the most important communication and security features of your Souvera account:

📧 MAIL
• All mailboxes in one place – including shared team mailboxes
• Push notifications for new e-mails
• Spam protection: move messages to the spam folder and permanently block senders with one tap
• Full-text search, folder management and calendar events from e-mails

📞 CALLING (TALK)
Souvera Workspace is also your phone app for your Souvera account: you receive and make voice and video calls with your contacts. For incoming calls, the app shows a full-screen ringing UI – even when the device is locked – so you never miss a call. For this ringing display only, the app uses the Android permission "USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT".
• Voice and video calls directly from the app
• Full-screen incoming-call notification
• Chats with images, files and voice messages

🛡️ SHIELD
• View and manage the spam quarantine
• Release messages or block senders
• Protection for personal and shared mailboxes

📅 CALENDAR & NOTES
• Calendar with day, week and month views
• Notes with server synchronisation

All data is stored on your own Souvera server (Nextcloud) in Germany – no tracking, no advertising.
